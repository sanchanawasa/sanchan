FAQ

Everything you actually want to know.

What is ZEC NINJA 888?

A collection of 888 unique chibi ninja characters inspired by Zcash culture, privacy, freedom and stealth. It is designed as a character universe first, not a logo-first collectible.

Why Zcash?

Because the cultural match is unusually strong: ninja imagery naturally communicates discretion and intentional visibility, while Zcash is widely associated with privacy-focused crypto technology.

What is the mint price?

TBD. We would rather announce the final price when launch mechanics are ready than publish a number too early and change it later.

How many are there?

888 total. The collection is male-ninja dominant, with a smaller number of kunoichi and a limited set of special and legendary variants.

What Zcash address should I submit?

Use your Zcash Unified Address — the address beginning with u1.... Never submit a seed phrase or private key.

Do I need to repost to join the WL?

Yes. Repost the official campaign post on X and paste the URL of your repost in the application form so we can verify it.

Does joining the WL guarantee a mint?

No. Applications may be reviewed and spots can be limited. Strong alignment with Zcash, collecting, community building or the project itself can help.

Are there rare traits?

Yes. The visual system includes core black/gold ninjas plus snow, oni, forest, cyber, sakura, fox, monk, colored variants and a small legendary tier.

Is the collection already minting?

Not yet. The project is currently in pre-launch mode. Whitelist is the best way to get positioned before mint details are announced.

Is this claiming every NFT transaction is private?

No. ZEC NINJA is inspired by Zcash privacy culture. Actual transaction visibility depends on the specific wallet, address type and mint/transfer flow used.

Why should I care this early?

Because the collection is being shaped before public launch. Early members are not arriving after the culture is established — they are the first layer of that culture.
